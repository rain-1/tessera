# ADR-0021: Independent review resources

- Status: Open. No reviewer is identified.
- Spec: §14 WP8, §16. Open question "independent review resources".

## Decision
No decision yet. The scope is fixed: the authority core, approval binding,
IPC, persistence, launcher and adapters (WP8). Until a reviewer is
identified, no release may claim independent review. Internal review
sign-off columns in `docs/traceability.md` stay empty.

## Consequences
- WP8 is blocked on this decision. P0–P6 are not.
